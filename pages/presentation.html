Hardware Setup

Actuation & Bus Architecture

Dual serial-bus servo system engineered for high joint torque, rapid grip response, and telemetry.

Actuation Strategy

Dual-Motor Pairing

  • ST3215 — Arm Joints High torque (30 kg·cm @ 12V, 45 RPM) for payload stability & link articulation.
  • ST3215-HS — Gripper High speed (106 RPM @ 12V, 20 kg·cm) for rapid pick-and-place response.
Topology & Interface

TTL Daisy Chain

  • Unified 3-Wire Bus Shared D / V / G line at 1 Mbps minimizes harness bulk & complexity.
  • Waveshare Adapter (A) CH343 USB-C bridge (9–12.6V, 5A peak) supporting synchronous control.
Precision & Health

Closed-Loop Telemetry

  • 12-Bit Resolution 4096 steps/360° (0.088° precision) with calibrated absolute zero.
  • Live State Monitoring Real-time feedback on position, velocity, load, voltage & temperature.

Safety Core • Dynamic Reactive Loop

3-Tier Reactive Safe Path Engine

Workstations are unpredictable: hands reach for coffee and phones move. The arm tracks a continuous 3D safety envelope with graded, real-time safety responses:

Tier 1: Dynamic Path Rerouting
Proximity > 15 cm

When a hand or obstacle enters the trajectory, the motion planner calculates a collision-free alternate spline in real-time (<15ms) to bypass the obstacle without pausing.

Tier 2: Adaptive Deceleration
Proximity 5–15 cm

If spatial clearance narrows, the controller throttles joint speed and torque by 60–80%, drastically reducing kinetic energy while maintaining precision micro-adjustments.

Tier 3: Compliant E-Stop & Backdrive
Proximity < 5 cm / Contact

Immediate sub-10ms motor stop. Joint torque drops into a compliant backdrivable floating state, eliminating pinch risks and preventing tipped-over drinks.
